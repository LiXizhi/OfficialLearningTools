六年级 · 英语How can I get there?

0–4 分钟 · 1 / 6

You have five minutes to find the museum

You are at the gate. You can see the park. Your partner can see the whole map. What will you ask?

Visitor: Excuse me. How can I get to the museum?
教师讲稿与观察点

老师用真实问路语气示范一次。听者背对地图，向看地图的同伴提问；提醒双方先约定朝向。

4–10 分钟 · 2 / 6

Where do I turn?

Read the two directions. Which one tells your partner exactly when to turn?

Choose and explain.

教师讲稿与观察点

让一位学生扮演只能听到指令的游客。第一次故意在起点右转，请说话人补上地标。

10–18 分钟 · 3 / 6

Guide your partner

Guide: Go straight to the park. Turn right there. Visitor: At the park? Follow the directions, one block at a time.

North ↑　Start at the gate, facing north.

教师讲稿与观察点

老师点名助手读访客台词，随后等待学生实际移动棋子。面向北出发，到公园后右转就是向东一步。

18–26 分钟 · 4 / 6

Repair a misunderstanding

Your partner is at the café instead of the museum. What might the visitor have heard?

Visitor: I turned right at the gate. Did you mean at the park?
Guide: Yes. Go back to the gate. Go straight to the park first.
教师讲稿与观察点

同伴交换角色，再走一次。教师只记录第一次转弯的地点、澄清语和最后落点。

26–34 分钟 · 5 / 6

A new destination

Start at the museum, facing north. Your new destination is the library. Give one landmark and check that your partner understands.

One possible route

Go straight to the school. Turn left at the school. The library is next to it.

教师讲稿与观察点

沿用上一页地图位置，在纸上从中心格出发。接受其他合乎地图的路线，要求包含转弯点。

34–40 分钟 · 6 / 6

Leave a voice script for the next pair

Switch roles. Write the exact words that helped your partner correct a wrong turn.

Guide: ____ at the ____.　 Visitor: Do you mean ____?　 End point: ____.
教师讲稿与观察点

听者独立到达才记为完成。只会齐读时，留下未澄清的原话。晚上比较两班样例，下次换地图再试。
